Research · Teaching · Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence for computer vision and medical image analysis.

My research focuses on deep learning, computer vision and medical imaging, with a particular interest in computational pathology, virtual staining, diffusion models and morphology-preserving image translation.

I also work on fairness and visual analysis in computer vision, and I am interested in developing reliable AI methods that can support biomedical research and clinical applications.

Biography

About me

I am currently an ATER at Université Côte d'Azur, where I teach computer science and data science courses while conducting research in artificial intelligence, computer vision and medical imaging.

I obtained my PhD in Computer Science in 2021. My doctoral research focused on person re-identification based on gait analysis. My current research explores generative models and diffusion-based approaches for computational pathology, particularly virtual staining from H&E to PAS and IHC.
